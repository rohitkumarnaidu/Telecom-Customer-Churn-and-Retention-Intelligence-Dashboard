# Business Requirements

## Business Problem
A telecommunications company is experiencing customer churn but lacks a consolidated view of customer contract behavior, tenure, monthly and total charges, subscribed services, payment methods, billing types, technical support, online security, and customer profile characteristics. The company needs an analytical dashboard that identifies high-churn groups and supports retention planning.

## Business Objectives
The project must answer key questions across several domains:
- **Executive**: Total customers, churn rates, average tenure, high-risk customer value.
- **Contract & Tenure**: Churn variation by contract duration and tenure, identifying high-risk tenure groups.
- **Pricing**: Churn by monthly charges and identifying high-charge, low-tenure risk.
- **Service**: Association of internet service, tech support, online security, and service combinations with churn.
- **Billing**: Churn by payment method, automatic vs. manual payments, and paperless billing.
- **Customer Profile**: Variation by demographics (senior, partner, dependents).
- **Retention**: Identifying high-risk segments, recommending retention actions, and assessing retained value.
